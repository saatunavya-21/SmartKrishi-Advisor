About Smart Krishi Market Advisor

Empowering Indian farmers through data-driven agricultural advisory, localized agro-meteorological forecasting, transparent mandi price discovery, and direct buyer linkages.

🌾 Our Mission

Smart Krishi Market Advisor is an advanced full-stack agritech digital platform built to bridge the information gap for modern farmers. In traditional agricultural ecosystems, farmers often encounter fragmented market pricing, volatile harvest realization, unpredictability in weather, and difficulties in identifying the most profitable crops for their specific soil profile.

Our mission is to eliminate market opacity, mitigate post-harvest distress sales, and provide scientifically validated, agronomically sound decision-support tools directly to farmers and agricultural stakeholders.

🎯 Core Objectives

🌾 Agronomic Advisory

Deliver tailored crop suggestions, multi-tier soil pH analysis, smart irrigation schedules, and plant disease diagnosis with bio-organic remedies.

💰 Fair Market Discovery

Provide benchmark mandi rates across districts, price comparison across markets, and statistical price trend forecasting to help farmers sell at peak value.

🤝 Direct Marketplace

Facilitate disintermediated connections between farmers and registered institutional/commercial buyers, reducing reliance on exploitative middlemen.

🏛️ Architecture & Governance

The system is architected using Spring Boot and MySQL, ensuring enterprise-grade transactional safety, cryptographic BCrypt credential protection, and responsive mobile-ready interfaces. All recommendations are derived from standard agronomic reference tables and genuine meteorology services (Open-Meteo API).
